person-m.glb, person-f.glb, skin-*.webp — built from two CC0 packs by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com):
  Universal Base Characters (Standard): the Superhero male/female bodies, faces, eyes, hair (Hair_SimpleParted,
  Hair_Long) and eyebrows; Universal Animation Library (Standard), Unreal-rig export: the eleven clips the room
  uses (idle, talking, walk, sit down, seated, seated talking, stand up, interact, pick up, dance), retargeted by
  bone name (rotations as they are; the pelvis's motion scaled to metres; other translations dropped so each
  body keeps its proportions). Clothes are painted onto the base colour by skinning weights (a bone belongs to a
  garment) — one skin per palette word. Tools: FBX2glTF, gltf-transform, PIL. CC0 1.0 Universal:
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

lounge-kit.glb — Kenney, Furniture Kit, https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit (via opengameart.org/content/furniture-kit)
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Seventeen pieces converted OBJ → glTF (obj2gltf), merged into one file with a
named node per piece (chairCushion, kitchenBar, stoolBar, loungeSofa, lampRoundFloor, rugRound, …), scaled to metres.
