person.glb, skin-*.png — Quaternius, CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
  the body, its clothes and its rig are "Animated Human" (https://opengameart.org/content/animated-human-low-poly);
  the ten clips it plays (idle, talking, walk, sit down, seated, seated talking, stand up, interact, pick up,
  dance) are the Universal Animation Library, RETARGETED onto that rig by bone name in the rest pose the two rigs
  actually hold — their frames differ by up to 158°, so a plain name-mapped copy makes a pretzel
  (scratch `ubc/retarget.mjs`, `restcmp.py`, `finish.mjs`). The outfit is a 32×32 palette the mesh's UVs point
  at, so a new outfit is a few hundred bytes and one recoloured swatch, not another body.

lounge-kit.glb — Kenney, Furniture Kit, https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit (via opengameart.org/content/furniture-kit)
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Seventeen pieces converted OBJ → glTF (obj2gltf), merged into one file with a
named node per piece (chairCushion, kitchenBar, stoolBar, loungeSofa, lampRoundFloor, rugRound, …), scaled to metres.

env.hdr, floor-parquet*.jpg, wall-plaster*.jpg, wood-dark*.jpg — Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license):
  env.hdr is "Billiard Hall" (https://polyhaven.com/a/billiard_hall), the 1k HDRI box-filtered to 256×128 and
  written as flat RGBE — the room's image-based light, never seen as a sky; the floor is "Herringbone Parquet"
  (diffuse, GL normal, roughness), the walls "Painted Plaster Wall" (diffuse, normal) and the tables "Dark Wood"
  (diffuse, normal), each the 1k JPEG resampled to 512 px.

person.glb, person-f.glb, person-tux.glb — Quaternius, CC0 1.0: the man is "Animated Men Pack"
(https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedmen.html), the woman "Animated Women Pack"
(https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedwomen.html), and the dealer's suit is the same men's pack. Each body
carries its OWN materials (Shirt, Skin, Pants, Shoes, Hair, Eyes as separate colours) and its OWN clips —
nothing is retargeted. The scratch `ubc/build-quat.mjs` renames the pack's clips into the room's vocabulary,
cuts the one long Sitting clip into the sit-down and the seated pose the room's state graph wants, and scales
the body to 1.78 m with its feet at y = 0 (`scripts/check-body.mjs` passes all three).
